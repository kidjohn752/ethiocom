Our Story

Founded with a passionate vision of bridging the gap between exceptional, time-honored local Ethiopian craftsmanship and global standards of e-commerce, Ethio-commerce provides a premium platform built for cultural authenticity without compromise.

Our platform directly supports community artisan unions, coffee farm cooperatives, and independent traditional weavers throughout Ethiopia, ensuring ethical trade practices while offering consumers unmatched luxury heritage goods across traditional apparel, organic spices, local instruments, and handcrafted decorative items.

Proud Official Cooperation with Telebirr

To deliver a genuinely seamless, secure, and frictionless digital transaction atmosphere, Ethio-commerce proudly operates in close strategic cooperation with Telebirr, Ethio Telecom's premier flagship mobile payment financial service ecosystem.

Through this tight native gateway integration, our valued patrons across the country can initiate automated instant mobile checkouts directly from their standard accounts, accelerating safety parameters, eliminating physical handling, and securing instant vendor payouts with state-of-the-art cryptographic banking protection protocols.
